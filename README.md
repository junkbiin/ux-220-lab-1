# My Favourite Thing [UX 220 - Lab 1]

Hello! My name is Sarah Addotey and my favourite thing that I have written has been a short monologue for my required drama class in high school!

It was about a girl who had to decide whether or not she wants to pursue her dream or continue to follow in her parents footsteps--running the family shop. It covers the pressures of fulfilling parental expectations while also attempting to live your own life to be self fulfilling.
